    Reservation guide

    How reservations work

    Four steps from booking to seeing the doctor.

    1. 1

      Choose a clinic and time

      Select a department, a date and an open 30-minute slot. Booked slots are greyed out.

    2. 2

      Get your queue number

      You receive a queue number and a private visit number. Logged-in patients also get a Patient ID (MRN).

    3. 3

      Track your wait

      Search your number on the Queue Status page. The wait is shown as a range, like 15–20 mins, because real visits vary.

    4. 4

      Arrive 10 minutes early

      Check in at the clinic, then wait for your turn. Can't make it? Cancel online to free the slot.

    Reminder

    How late can I cancel?

    Up to 30 minutes before your appointment. After that the slot can't be reused, so online cancellation closes. Call (045) 982-0110 instead.

    What do I need to bring?

    A valid school or government ID, and your queue or visit number. Bring any previous medical records if you have them.

    Do I need an account?

    Yes. An account is required so we can verify each booking and prevent fake or prank reservations. It also gives you a Patient ID (MRN) and keeps your appointment history in one place.

    Why is the wait a range?

    Consultations run shorter or longer than planned, so we show a window such as 15–20 mins rather than a number we can't promise.

    What are the clinic hours?

    Mon–Fri, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM. Each department has its own days and hours, listed above.
