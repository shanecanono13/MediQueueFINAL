About MediQueue

Built to take the line out of the clinic visit

MediQueue started as a Group 5 case study (NA-3B) at Tarlac State University. Students and staff were queuing at the San Isidro campus clinic just to find out how long the wait would be.

Patients reserve a slot, receive a queue number and check progress online. The clinic gets an orderly schedule, shorter lines and data on which services are in demand.
