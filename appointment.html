Pre-Appointment Terms

  • Arrive 10 minutes before your selected time.
  • Each slot is 30 minutes, so doctors have a breather between patients.
  • Estimated time with the doctor is 15–30 minutes.
  • Check your status or cancel from the Queue Status page. Cancelling closes 30 minutes before your appointment.
  • Bring a valid ID for verification at the clinic.
